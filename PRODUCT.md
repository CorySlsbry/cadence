# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Short-form creators and founder-creators (TikTok/Reels/Shorts), including recruited affiliate creators (5k–500k followers). They are mid-task: picking what to film, recording a take, customizing a render, downloading clips. Many are non-technical "first-timers" recording straight from the teleprompter. A second surface (the logged-out landing page) is brand register — design IS the product there.

## Product Purpose

CloutHook (repo codename "Cadence") is the full-loop creator OS: it finds the proven video in the user's niche (outlier intelligence), scripts it in their voice (Ikigai brand blueprint + 7-part hook), scrolls it on the teleprompter while they record, then cleans, grades, captions, b-rolls, clips, and ranks the result into studio-grade 9:16 shorts. Success = a user ships a better short in minutes without an editor, and trusts the tool enough to pay monthly (Free/Base $30/Creator $79; per-scene tokens, no expiry).

## Brand Personality

Premium, calm, inevitable. "Studio-grade" confidence — sells Relief, Authority, and Speed, never price. Peer-to-peer voice (never order-taker, never hype). Dark, glowing, precise: deep indigo-black surfaces with a blue→violet brand gradient used sparingly as the accent of authority.

## Anti-references

- OpusClip's pain points: credit anxiety, expiring clips, black-box "virality scores", renders that hang. CloutHook explains (score + reason), meters honestly (per-scene tokens), and never breaks silently.
- 2026 AI-slop SaaS: cream/sand defaults, gradient-text everywhere, identical icon-card grids, fake urgency banners, cheap scarcity timers.
- Mass-market video editors (CapCut-style toolbars): CloutHook is opinionated automation with a few decisive controls, not a timeline editor.

## Design Principles

1. **The tool disappears into the take.** Recording and reviewing are the hero moments; chrome stays quiet, controls read at a glance.
2. **Explain, don't mystify.** Every AI judgment ships with its "why" (clip scores, outlier analysis, render decisions).
3. **Decisive defaults, few controls.** One good path pre-selected; customization is progressive disclosure, never a wall of toggles.
4. **State is sacred.** Every async surface has real loading/empty/error/success states; progress is truthful (no fake bars).
5. **Premium = restraint + one glow.** The brand gradient appears at moments of payoff (primary actions, scores, finished renders), never as decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on dark surfaces (body ≥4.5:1 — already enforced via global legibility overrides). Full keyboard operability with visible focus; `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for every animation (existing global reduce block must stay comprehensive). Captions-first product DNA carries into the UI: never rely on color alone for state.
