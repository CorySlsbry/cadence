## 1. The name
**CloutHook** = **Clout** (the creator's reach/influence/win) + **Hook** (the scroll-stopping first 1.5s — the biggest lever in short-form). The name *is* the product: CloutHook finds the viral moment and writes the hook that earns the clout. Spoken as one word; written `CloutHook` in prose.

## 2. Positioning
**For** creators, short-form marketers, and agencies **who** live or die by the first second, **CloutHook is** the AI editor **that** finds the hook-worthy moment and writes the line that stops the scroll — **unlike** generic clip tools that just chop long video into dumb segments.
**One-liner:** *Hook first. Clout follows.*

## 3. Taglines
- **Primary:** Hook first. Clout follows.
- Find the moment. Land the hook. · Stop the scroll. · The hook that earns the clout.

## 4. Voice
Bold · high-energy · creator-native · confident — **premium, never cringe**.
- **Do:** short declarative sentences, verbs first, specific outcomes ("three hooks in 30 seconds").
- **Don't:** hype-bro clickbait, 🔥-walls, fake scarcity, generic AI-speak, em-dash soup.

## 5. Visual direction — **Botanical Glow**
Inspired by Sabrina Ramonov's dark-purple video frame (the vertical rectangle shown on cutaways), but made original and ownable: **vivid red + purple flowering plants on green weaving stems, lit with a premium glow, over a deep gradient-violet field.** The hero artifact is a **9:16 frame** dropped in when the video cuts from talking; the same botanical system scales to the logo, wreath monogram, end-card, avatar, and app splash.

Pillars: **floral** (red/violet blooms), **organic line** (green stems weaving the border), **glow** (soft bloom on every element), **gradient** (crimson→violet), **premium restraint** (negative space, refined type).
